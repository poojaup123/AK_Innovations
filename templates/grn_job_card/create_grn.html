What is a GRN?

A Goods Receipt Note (GRN) records the receipt of materials from outsourced work. Once created, it will update inventory levels and mark the job card as completed.

Next Steps:

  • GRN will be created with the job card quantity
  • Inventory will be updated automatically
  • Job card status will change to "Completed"
  • Material will be available for next process
