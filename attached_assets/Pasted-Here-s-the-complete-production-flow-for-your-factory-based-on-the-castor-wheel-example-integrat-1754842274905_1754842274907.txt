## **🏭 Complete Production Flow — Castor Wheel Example**

---

### **Step 1 — Material Procurement**

* **We buy MS Sheets** from vendors
* GRN (3-step workflow) is created when material arrives
* Batch ID is generated for each received lot
* Inventory updated in **Raw Material** stock
* GST & accounting entries made automatically

---

### **Step 2 — Sub-Assembly 1: Mounted Plate**

* **Process**: Cutting → Notching → Zinc Plating
* **Job Type**:

  * **Cutting** = In-house (tracked in production)
  * **Notching & Zinc** = Outsourced (tracked via Job Work + GRN)
* **Flow**:

  1. Production order raised from BOM
  2. Cutting done internally — inventory updated
  3. Notching & Zinc sent to vendors (Job Work Out)
  4. Vendor returns processed plates → GRN created → Inventory updated in **WIP/Finished**

---

### **Step 3 — Sub-Assembly 2: Base Plate (Phantom BOM)**

* **Process**: Cutting → Zinc Plating
* Phantom BOM = No storage in inventory
* Same steps as Mounted Plate, but:

  * Directly consumed in final product assembly after zinc
  * No final inventory entry for "Base Plate" — materials flow to Castor Wheel assembly immediately

---

### **Step 4 — Other Components**

* Wheel, Rings, Balls, Rivets are purchased directly (with GRN)
* No manufacturing process
* Inventory updated as Raw Material

---

### **Step 5 — Final Assembly**

* All components come together:

  * Mounted Plate (Finished)
  * Base Plate (Processed via Phantom BOM)
  * Ring
  * Balls (16 pcs)
  * Wheel
  * Wheel Rivet
  * Small Rivet
* **Assembly Process**: In-house job → Production module updates stock from WIP to Finished Goods

---

### **Step 6 — Packing**

* 10 castor wheels in **1 LD bag**
* 10 LD bags in **1 carton**
* Packing materials (LD bags, cartons) pulled from inventory as per BOM
* Finished goods stock updated with packaged quantities

---

### **Step 7 — Accounting Integration**

Every stage auto-generates accounting entries:

* GRN = Purchase + GST Input + GRN Clearing
* Job Work GRN = Job Work expense + Vendor Payable
* In-house process = Labor & Overhead allocation
* Final assembly = Inventory Valuation update
