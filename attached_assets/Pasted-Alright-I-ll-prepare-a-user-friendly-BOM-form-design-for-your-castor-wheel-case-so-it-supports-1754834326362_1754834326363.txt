## **💡 User Flow**

1. **Create Final Product BOM** (Castor Wheel)
2. **Define Sub-Assemblies** (Mounted Plate BOM, Base Plate Phantom BOM)
3. **Attach Processes** to each component
4. **Mark Phantom BOM** where needed (Base Plate)
5. **Link Vendors** for outsourced processes
6. **System Auto-calculates** material requirement, cost, and process lead time
7. **Production Order uses BOM** to trigger job work, GRN (if outsourced), and batch updates automatically
